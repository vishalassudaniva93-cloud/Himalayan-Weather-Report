What to expect in October (general guidance)

  • Skies: Post-monsoon weeks are generally clearer, giving the best mountain views of the year, though isolated showers are possible early in the month.
  • Temperatures: Days are mild in valleys but nights drop sharply with altitude. Ladakh and high-camp treks can see sub-zero nights.
  • Daylight: Days are shortening, so plan earlier starts on high-altitude drives and treks.
These are typical seasonal patterns, not a forecast for specific dates.

Agency action checklist

  • Re-check IMD and local road/flight updates 48 hours and again 12 hours before departure.
  • Build 1–2 buffer days into Uttarakhand, Sikkim and Ladakh itineraries.
  • Advise clients on layered clothing, rain protection, and altitude acclimatization.
  • Confirm travel insurance covers weather disruptions and evacuation.
  • Share local emergency and operator contacts with every group.
